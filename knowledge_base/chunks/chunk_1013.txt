MARATHWADA	REGION	
Marathwada region of Maharashtra is comprised of Aurangabad, Jalna, Beed, 
Latur, Osmanabad, Hingoli, Parbhani, Nanded. The region has moderate rainfall 
(600-800 mm) and has medium to deep black soils. This region possesses state 
agricultural university viz., Dr. Vasantrao Naik Marathwada Krishi Vidyapeeth, 
Parbhani.
