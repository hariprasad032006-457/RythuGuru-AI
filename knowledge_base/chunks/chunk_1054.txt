Irrigation management should be done early in the morning, at evening or during night

Brinjal
If incidence of shoot and fruit borer observed in Brinjal, collect and destroy 
caterpillar or Spray Neemark 4 % or Cypermethrin 25% EC 5 ml or Chlorpyriphos 20 
EC 20 ml or  Deltamethrin 1 % or Traizophos 20 ml per 10 litres of water.
Turmeric
Harvesting, boiling, drying, polishing operations are being carried out in turmeric crop. 
To save the crop from unseasonal rains or hailstorm, crop should not be stored in open 
place. The harvested turmeric crop should be covered by plastic paper so that it should 
not be damaged by rains.
