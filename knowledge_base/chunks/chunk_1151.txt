FRUIT		CROPS
Fruit crops like pineapple, banana, papaya, dragon fruit, citrus, and mango can be 
planted from May to July in pits, with mango and banana requiring pit sizes of 60 x 60 
x 60 cm, citrus needing 75 x 75 x 75 cm, papaya requiring 45 x 45 x 45 cm, and 
pineapple being planted in trenches or raised beds instead of pits. Pit preparation 
should be done 15-20 days before planting. After digging, leave the pits open for a few 
weeks to expose the soil to sunlight. Fill the pits with a 1:1 mix of soil and compost. 
Regular hand weeding or the use of mulch (e.g., straw or black polythene) is 
recommended for pineapple. For bananas, bamboo stakes may be used to prevent 
lodging from strong winds and rain. For cucurbit crops, trellising with bamboo or 
nylon support helps reduce disease and improves fruit quality.
