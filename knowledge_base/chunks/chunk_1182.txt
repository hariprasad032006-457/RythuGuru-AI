Implement strict measures for African Swine Fever (ASF) by quarantining infected 
farms, prohibiting the movement of animals and personnel between farms, and 
ensuring the proper disposal of carcasses (deep burial with lime/bleaching powder). 
Avoid swill feeding unless properly boiled; maintain proper records of animal health 
and visitors; and prohibit animal movement between farms to reduce disease risk. 
Report suspected ASF cases to veterinary authorities immediately.
