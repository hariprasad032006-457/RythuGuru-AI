Piggery
To maintain healthy and productive pig herds, farmers should implement strict 
biosecurity measures by regularly cleaning pens every 2-3 days using 1% 
sodium/calcium hydroxide or 2% caustic soda solution, while disinfecting all 
equipment and vehicles with 0.03% sodium hypochlorite, 1% formaldehyde, or 
Virkon S/B-904 (1:100 dilution). Farm workers must disinfect their shoes, clothes, 
and tools before contact with pigs, and animals should be provided with cooling 
water baths during peak heat along with clean drinking water refreshed at least 
thrice daily. Critical health interventions include vaccinating against Swine Fever by 
the 3rd week of May, administering Ivermectin (200 mcg/kg BW) and Albendazole 
(5-10 mg/kg BW) for deworming, giving piglets 1ml iron injections on days 4 and 14, 
and supplementing feed with Ascal (10ml twice daily for 10 days). Nutritional
