25-30 days after fruit set provide physical protection against fruit nut borer, sun 
burning, cracking and also improves pericarp color and fruit quality. To manage litchi 
nut borer follow clean cultivation by removing fallen fruits (mummies) from the 
orchard and ploughing destroys the carry-over of pests to the next crop.
