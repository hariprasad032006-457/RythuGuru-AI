Silage	Preparation:	Produce silage from excess green fodder such as maize, bajra, 
and sorghum to guarantee feed supply during scarcity. Small-scale farmers can 
utilize polypropylene bags and drums for silage preparation. It is advisable to feed 
silage post-milking to prevent any smell in the milk.
