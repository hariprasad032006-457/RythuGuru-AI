based on the soil testing report. The farmers need to adequate quantity of gypsum

brings to pH toward neutral range. It also helps in increasing the availability of plant 
nutrients such as sulphur and calcium. Gypsum is the cheapest source of sulphur and 
calcium. As Rajasthan is major R&M and Groundnut growing state, the increased 
availability of sulphur due to gypsum application will also enhance the yields of R&M 
in following season. The improvement in pH also helps in increasing the availability 
of all plant nutrients leading to higher production and quality improvement in kharif 
crops especially in oilseed and pulses.
