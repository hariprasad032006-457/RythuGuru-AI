Application	Schedule:	Apply the full dose of P, K, Zn, and one-third of N at the time of 
puddling. The remaining two-thirds of N should be top-dressed in two equal splits at 
3 and 6 weeks after transplanting. Incorporate 15 tons of well-decomposed FYM/ha 
before transplanting.
Plant	Protection:	Fall armyworm: Spray the crop with chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 
200 ml or spinetoram 11.7 SC 250 ml or emamectin benzoate 5 SG 200 g in 500 litres 
of water per ha, for crop up to 20 days old. Maize stem borer: Apply Phorate 10 G or 
carbofuran 3% granules @ 7.5 kg per ha in the shoot of the plant after 16 to 30 days
