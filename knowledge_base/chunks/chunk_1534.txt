Pomegranate
Planting	Material:	Tissue culture saplings are the best to guarantee disease-free 
planting material and for planting in new areas where there are no pomegranate 
orchards. 
Water	Management:	Light irrigation within 3-4 days of sowing if no rainfall. First 
full irrigation can be given around 20-30 days after sowing. In water-scarce areas, 
use drip irrigation and mulching to retain moisture.
