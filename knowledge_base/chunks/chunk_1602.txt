done. While making the beds, neem or Karanja cake should be added in the beds. 
Along with this, a trench of 1.5-2 feet should be made around the polyhouse. It can 
also be controlled with carbofuran. Powdery mildew: It can be controlled by spraying 
1 ml of hydrophilic sulfur or karathane. Leaf curl and leaf bunch disease: For its 
control, the sap sucking pests should be controlled before the disease appears. 
Mosaic: This disease is also spread by sucking insects; hence these insects should be 
controlled timely. Wilt disease: Drenching with 1% solution of Ridomil Gold should 
be done.
