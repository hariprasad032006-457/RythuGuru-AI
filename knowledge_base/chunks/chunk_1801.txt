Mung bean and urd bean should be sown with the onset of monsoon, typically from 
June to July, as both early and late sowings can negatively aﬀect crop performance. 
Early sowing may lead to maturity during the rainy period, causing losses, while late 
sowing results in poor crop growth and higher disease incidence. During the rainy 
season, both crops are often broadcast using a seed rate of 15 kg/ha, but line sowing 
is recommended as it provides better canopy management and facilitates 
intercultural operations.
