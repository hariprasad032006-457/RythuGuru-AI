During the monsoon season, maintain clean, leak-proof animal sheds to prevent 
dampness and disease. Rainy-season grasses should be partially sun-dried before 
feeding to reduce water content and improve digestibility. De-worm livestock at the 
beginning of the season and repeat as necessary since worm burden increases in 
moist conditions. Regular spraying of animals for ectoparasite control and cutting of 
bushes near sheds is recommended. Disinfect sheds frequently and ensure dry, safe 
feed storage. In goat husbandry, avoid feeding potentially toxic plants like 
rhododendrons, and seek immediate veterinary help if ingested. Goats are highly 
susceptible to internal and external parasites and must be dewormed every 2 to 3 
months. Young kids should receive deworming medicine at 7-8 weeks of age. CDT 
vaccination should also be administered at 7-8 weeks, with a booster after 4 weeks
