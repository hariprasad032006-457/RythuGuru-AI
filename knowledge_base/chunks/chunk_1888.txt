To control Peach leaf curl, adopt integrated approach. Thin the fruits, ensure 
moisture availability, apply organic fertilizers with high N content to reduce stress of 
plant.  A copper-based fungicide i.e. Copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 600 g per 200 
liters of water should be sprayed. Time of spray is falling of leaves and bud swell 
stage.  For the control of peach leaf curl aphis spray imidacloprid 17.8% SL@ 
80ml/200 L. of water  For the control of gummosis in stone fruits spray 
Streptocycline 20 g + Copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 600 g per 200 litres of water.
