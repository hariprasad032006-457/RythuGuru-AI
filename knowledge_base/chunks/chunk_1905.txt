Potato
Recommended Varieties of potato for hills are Kufri Himalini and Kufri Jyoti with seed 
rate of 1.5 to 2.5 t/ha. Recommended crop geometry for potato cultivation is 50 cm X 
20 cm. During last ploughing around 10-15 tonne of FYM plus basal dose of 90 Kg 
Urea, 300 Kg SSP and 65 kg MOP is recommended to be applied followed by top 
dressing of Urea at the rate of 70 kg/ha and 50 kg/ha 30 and 50 DAS in potato
