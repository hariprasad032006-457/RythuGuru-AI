During the rainy season in Uttarakhand, mushroom production requires careful 
management to ensure healthy growth and prevent contamination in mushroom 
units. Mushrooms thrive in a humid environment (80-90%), but excessive humidity 
during the rainy season can become a challenge; hence, proper ventilation is crucial
