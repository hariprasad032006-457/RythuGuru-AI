Disease	 Surveillance	 during	 monsoon	 season: Livestock are prone to various 
viral, fungal, protozoan, bacterial infection. However, vaccines are available for 
prevention of some of the economically important diseases such as FMD, PPR, H.S. 
etc. So, vaccination of livestock should be done prior to monsoon season. If diagnosed 
with disease the animal should be isolated and prompt treatment should be done. 
Diﬀerent ectoparasites act as vector for disease transmission such as Theleriosis, 
Babesiosis, Trypanosomiasis etc. thus ecto-parasiticidal drugs/acaricides should be 
applied at regular interval. In case of any disease outbreak, restricted movement of 
animal, culling of aﬀected animal, proper disposal of carcass and disinfection of shed
