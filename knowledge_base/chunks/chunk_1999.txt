Treat soil with formalin at 1:50 ratio at least 30 days prior to planting. Treat vine 
cuttings by soaking in Streptocyclin solution (1 g/4 L of water) for 25 min followed 
treatment with 0.5% Bordeaux mixture.  Select upland or medium up-land with good 
drainage facility. Saplings or vine cuttings should be planted in broad raised bed to 
avoid or minimize damage of planted seedling during heavy rain. Use 50% agro-shade 
net to maintain favourable micro-climate. Prune vines at 2 m height. Maintain boroj 
height of 2.5 m and apply soil rings at base after 2 m growth. 
Keep rhizosphere clean and ensure good drainage facility to reduce chance of collar
