Agriculture remains the cornerstone of India's economy, supporting nearly half of 
the population and providing employment, nutrition, and rural development. The 
Kharif season, spanning from June to October, is particularly vital due to its reliance 
on the south-west monsoon. Approximately 55% of India's net sown area is rainfed, 
making the timely and equitable distribution of monsoon rains crucial for 
agricultural success.
