The current weather is favorable for onion cultivation. Before sowing, treat the seeds 
with Captan 75 WS @ 2.5 g/kg seed. In transplanted onion, dip the onion roots in a 
solution of Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% @ 2.5 g/L of water prior to 
transplanting. Raise the onion nursery with high-yielding varieties such as Agrifound 
Dark Red, Pusa Ridhi, N-53, Bhima Super, Bhima Raj, and Bhima Dark Red @ 12-15 kg 
seeds/ha. Considering the current temperature, farmers can begin garlic sowing. 
Ensure that the soil has adequate moisture before sowing. Recommended improved
