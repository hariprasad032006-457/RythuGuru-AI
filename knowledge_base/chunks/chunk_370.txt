Cotton cultivation thrives best in well-drained loamy to sandy loam soils. Prior to 
sowing, deep ploughing is essential along with the incorporation of 10-15 t/ha of 
farmyard manure (FYM) to enhance soil fertility. Sowing is typically carried out in June-
July under rainfed conditions and during April-May for irrigated crops. Recommended 
spacing for normal planting is 90-120 × 30-45 cm, while under high-density planting 
(HDP) systems, 60 × 30 cm spacing is advised. Suitable varieties include Bt hybrids like 
G. Cot. Hy. 24 and 26, and desi types such as GADC-3 and GADC-4. The seed rate varies by
