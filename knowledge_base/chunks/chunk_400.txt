Weed management can be achieved using pre-emergence herbicides such as Atrazine 
at 1 kg/ha or Metribuzin at 1.25 kg/ha. During dry spells, provide light irrigation, 
particularly at the formative and grand growth stages, to maintain adequate moisture 
for proper tillering. Earthing up should be carried out at 45-60 days after planting 
(DAP) for partial earthing and at 90-120 DAP for full earthing to promote root growth
