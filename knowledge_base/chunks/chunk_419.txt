Maize
For transplanted rice cultivation, use 20-25 days old seedlings, with 2-3 plants per 
hill. Treat seedlings with Azotobacter  and PSB liquid before transplanting. Irrigate 
with 5-7 cm of stagnant water. For top dressing, apply 40-48 kg N/ha at the tillering 
stage. For plant protection, control stemborer with light traps and spray 
Flubendiamide or Indoxacarb. Manage Leaf Blight with Streptocycline + Copper 
Oxychloride, and control Blast with Tricyclazole spray.
Soybean
Use	recommended	varieties:	NRC-37, JS-335, KDS 726, Gujarat Soybean-2, and JS-
9305. The ideal sowing time is from June to mid-July, with early sowing if conditions 
permit. Land preparation involves applying 10-15 tonnes of manure, followed by 
ploughing and harrowing. For spacing, use 45x7.5 cm or 30x5 cm, with a seed rate of 
70-80 kg/ha.
