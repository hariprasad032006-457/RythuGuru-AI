Tomato
For tomato cultivation, recommended varieties include Gujarat Tomato-2, Gujarat 
Tomato-5, and Anand Tomato-8. The seed rate is 200-250 g/ha, and the ideal 
spacing is 90x75 cm or 70x60 cm. For nursery management, apply Metalaxil MZ to 
prevent root rot and use micro-nutrient sprays. For plant protection, control Leaf 
Miner with Neem powder/oil spray, and use Spinosad for severe infestations. 
Manage Early Blight with Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil spray.
