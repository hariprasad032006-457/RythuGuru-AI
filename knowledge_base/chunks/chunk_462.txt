Method	of	sowing:	Sowing should be done preferably in rows at a spacing of 30 cm

Time	of	sowing:	Optimum time of sowing of groundnut is month of June. Under 
irrigated conditions, sowing should be completed by second fortnight of June, 
whereas under rainfed conditions sowing at the onset of monsoon should be done. 
Sowing of crop after 15 July should be avoided.
