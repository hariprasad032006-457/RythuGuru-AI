Cultural Practices along with Fertilizer application: Varieties, K-75, Solan Srijan are 
recommended for open cultivation of cucumber while seedless varieties Him Palam 
Kheera-1 and Him Palam Kheera-2 are suitable for protected cultivation. Australian 
Green, Pusa Alankar and Solan Aadvik varieties of summer squash  have been 
recommended for cultivation. Nursery of cucurbits should be produced in polybags 
or plug trays for healthy nursery production. Cucurbits planting may be completed 
by April end using FYM and Fertilizers (NPK @ 100:50:60) before sowing with 
Nitrogen in 3 split doses at 30, 45, 70 days after transplanting.
