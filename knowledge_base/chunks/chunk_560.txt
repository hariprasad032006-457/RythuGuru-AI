The monsoon period predisposes animals to a surge in diseases such as foot 
rot, mastitis, pneumonia, and enterotoxemia due to high humidity and wet 
housing. Livestock should be actively monitored for symptoms of Lumpy Skin 
Disease (LS), vector-borne conditions like theileriosis, babesiosis, and 
anaplasmosis. Prompt diagnosis and early reporting are crucial. In case of 
animal mortality, carcasses must be disposed through deep burial (at least 4 
feet) with lime layering to prevent environmental contamination and disease 
transmission.
Swine units must ensure annual vaccination against Classical Swine Fever. 
Nutritional care of breeding sows includes daily supplementation with 60-80 mL of 
calcium and 50-60 g of mineral mixture. Pigs should have access to wallowing areas 
or showers for thermal relief. Deworm
