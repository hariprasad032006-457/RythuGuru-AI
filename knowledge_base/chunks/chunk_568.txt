Swine units must ensure annual vaccination against Classical Swine Fever. 
Nutritional care of breeding sows includes daily supplementation with 60-80 mL of 
calcium and 50-60 g of mineral mixture. Pigs should have access to wallowing areas 
or showers for thermal relief. Deworming using combination drugs like Levamisole 
and Albendazole should be conducted quarterly.
