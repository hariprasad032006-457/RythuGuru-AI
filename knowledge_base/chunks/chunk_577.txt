For pests, apply chlorpyriphos 1.5% D (10 kg/acre) in last ploughing, use light 
(2/acre) & pheromone traps (6/acre), spray methyl demeton, cypermethrin or 
cartap hydrochloride in whorls. For Blister Beetle, Cut Worm, Stem Borer & Army 
Worm in maize, use acetamiprid 20SP @ 0.2 g/L or chlorpyriphos 20EC @ 480 
ml/acre, apply chlorpyriphos 1.5% D @ 10 kg/acre in soil for cut worm, cartap 
hydrochloride 4G @ 8 kg/acre in whorls for stem borer, and adopt trap cropping 
(okra/arhar) and manual beetle removal for blister beetle. For smuts, treat seed 
with carbendazim/carboxin @ 2 g/kg; for stalk rot, drench with copper 
oxychloride 3 g/L or apply bleaching powder @ 10 kg/acre; for leaf blight, spray 
propiconazole @ 0.1% at disease onset.
