Nutrient	Management	for	Rice	Nursery:	Apply 275 g of urea, 450 g of DAP, and 200 g 
of MOP per Marla (25 m²) area of nursery bed. These fertilizers should be incorporated 
into the soil during bed preparation. Additionally, apply 1 kg of nitrogen (N) per 100 m² 
of nursery bed area 6-10 days before uprooting the seedlings. Nutrient Management 
for Main Field (Transplanting). Apply well-decomposed compost or farmyard manure 
(FYM) at the rate of 15-20 tons per hectare. Incorporate this into the soil during land 
preparation. Application of vermicompost @ 2.5 t/ha can replace 5 t FYM/ ha and 25% 
of the recommended NPK dose.
