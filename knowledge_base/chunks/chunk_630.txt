Pre-sowing	Seed	Treatments:	In order to minimize the chances of disease spread 
through seed, it is better to soak the seeds for overnight and at transplantation  dip 
the seedlings roots in either of the following fungicides before sowing: Tricyclazole 
75 WP @ 60 g/100 liter water, Ediphenphos 50 EC @ 100 ml/100 liter water,The 
sprouted seeds should not be treated with any fungicide as it may damage the 
plumule/ radicle.
