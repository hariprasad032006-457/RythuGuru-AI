Diﬀerent	stages	of	harvesting
Green	stage:	Fully mature green fruits are harvested to get more price of the produce 
at early stage. Breaker or turning stage: The fruits are harvested when 1/4 th 
blossom end of the fruit show pink colour. This is suitable to send the fruits to long 
distances.	
Integrated	Nutrient	Management:	Application of vermicompost @ 4.5t/ha along 
with 50% RFD (75:50:50) is recommended to obtain higher tuber yields. For 
supplementing Sulphur requirements Copper Sulphate@10kg/ ha is recommended.
