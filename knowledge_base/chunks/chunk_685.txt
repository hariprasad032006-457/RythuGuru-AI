Bhindi	(Okra)
Pusa Sawani (60-80 q/ ha) is Recommended variety of okra for Kashmir valley. Seeds 
pre-soaked in hot water at 50°C for 30 minutes and directly sown on the ridge tops 
which are 15 -20 cm high above ground and 45 cm apart can be sown in Late April- 
May with seed rate of 15-20 kg/ha. After the seeds have germinated and plant has 
attained height of about 7-10 cm, plants are thinned to a spacing of 30cm between 
plants. NPK @120:90:60 can be applied.
