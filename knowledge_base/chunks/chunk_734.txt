Mango varieties such as Amrapali, Dussehri, and Neelanchal Kesari should be irrigated 
at weekly intervals to ensure proper fruit growth and development. For quality fruit 
production, apply 500 grams of urea and 400 grams of muriate of potash per plant. 
During the fruit development stage, spray micronutrients on the leaves. After 
harvesting, provide adequate nutrition to the plants to restore their vitality. With the 
onset of the monsoon, sow green manure crops such as 20 kg of hemp or 10 kg of 
dhaincha per acre. Incorporate manure and compost into the soil 45 days after sowing. 
Pruning of mango trees should be carried out in the months of December and January.
