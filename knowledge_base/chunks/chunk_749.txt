For pig units, farmers are advised to grow green fodder and add 40% green matter with 
60% concentrated fodder, such as rice husk, maize, wheat husk, oil cake, mineral mixture, 
and salt. To prepare concentrated feed, mix 60% rice husk, 20% ground corn, 20% oil 
cake, and 20% rice bran. Also add 10% wheat bran, 8% salt, and 1.5% vitamin and 
mineral mixture. Providing extra immunity boosters like zinc and selenium in the feed is 
recommended. Ensure pigs are vaccinated against common monsoon-related diseases
