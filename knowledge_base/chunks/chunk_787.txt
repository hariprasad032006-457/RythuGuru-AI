Karnataka
Commonly grown onion varieties include Arka Kalyan, Arka Niketan, Arka Pragati, 
Bhima Super, and Bhima Red, while in the Gadag region, Bhima Super is the most 
preferred variety. The recommended sowing seasons across both regions are June-
July, September-October, and January-February, depending on the crop type. For 
transplanting, 8-12 kg of seed per hectare is required, while seed drill sowing needs 
20-25 kg/ha. In bulb planting, approximately 875 kg of bulbs per hectare is used. A 
general seed rate of 5-6 kg/ha is recommended when using Bhima Super or Arka 
Kalyan varieties. Before sowing, seed treatment with Captan 80 WP at 2 g/kg of seed 
helps control fungal diseases, and Imidacloprid 60 FS at 15 ml/kg of seed is essential 
for protection against sucking pests. The recommended fertilizer dose is 30 tonnes of 
FYM per hectare, along with 125:75:125 kg/ha of N:P:K fertilizers. In Belagavi,
