For nutrient management, hybrids require 250:250:250 kg/ha of NPK, while 
varieties require 115:100:60 kg/ha. Seeds are treated with Trichoderma subtilis @ 
50 g/kg or Thiram @ 1.25 g/kg for protection against seed-borne diseases. The crop 
is planted on ridges and furrows (90 × 45 cm) or raised beds (75 × 45 cm) for rainy 
season crops. Foliar nutrition with Vegetable Special @ 3 g/l is applied fortnightly. 
Chilli
