Soil	Acidity	Management:	It is a critical factor in pepper cultivation, especially in 
the lateritic soils of Kerala that tend to be acidic. Application of lime @ 500 grams per 
vine need to be done after receiving rainfall. It will help to reduce the incidence of 
fungal diseases too. Liming helps to neutralize soil acidity, enhances nutrient 
availability, and promotes better uptake of essential elements by the plant roots.
