Drought management in homesteads should be undertaken through husk burial in 
trenches, mulch with dried coconut leaves in coconut basins. Plant intercrops like 
tubers, vegetables, perennial vegetables, medicinal plants, spices and banana to 
make each homestead as nutritional garden.
