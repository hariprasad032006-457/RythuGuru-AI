Select healthy suckers, dry them in direct sunlight for 3-4 days, dip in cow dung slurry 
and ash to prevent rhizome weevil, and periodically remove dried leaves to maintain 
clean cultivation. Prune the side suckers every month which attracts rhizome weevil 
and pseudo stem weevil. Proper disposal oﬀ the banana plant after harvesting and do 
not dump infested materials into manure pit. Apply neem cake @ 1 kg/plant to 
prevent rhizome and pseudo stem weevil infestations, plant until May, ensure proper
