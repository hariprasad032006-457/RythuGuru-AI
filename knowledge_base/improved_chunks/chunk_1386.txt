Training	and	Pruning:	The training and pruning of plants is started at 40-50 days. 
The excess branches of plants should be removed and only 2-3 branches per plant 
should be kept. The plants should be trained by twine or plastic ropes. While pruning, 
the adjacent branches and weak branches are removed and shoots coming below the 
main branches should be removed from time to time. Training of the branches of the 
plants is necessary once a week, the new growth is wrapped with twine and kept 
high. If training is not done on time, the branches break, and the quality of the 
produce gets aﬀected.
