Poultry
Sheep	and	Goat
Poultry are very sensitive to heat, so protect the birds from overcrowding and direct 
environmental heat and take measures to protect the chickens from hot winds. To 
protect chickens from heat, gunny bags/sacks should be planted around the farm, 
and these gunny bags /sacks should be wetted by spraying water through sprinklers 
in the morning, evening and as required. Proper drinking water should be available 
to chicks. To reduce the heat stress in poultry, supplement the vitamin C in diet. 
Maintain the proper ventilation in poultry house. Keep the curtain open in the night. 
Deworming of chicks should be carried out with piprazine mixed in drinking water.
