Tying	and	Wrapping: These operations are most essential in sugarcane cultivation 
just to provide mechanical support to the grown-up plants to prevent lodging. The 
leaves are removed from the plants and wrapped together by taking all canes in one 
bundle. Tying should be done in the month of August when cane reaches about 2 m 
height. Green leaves should not be tied up together while tying up the crop.
