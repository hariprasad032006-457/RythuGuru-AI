Ginger
For management of Rhizome rot seed can be treated with PBAT-3 like Carbendazim, 
Mancozeb, Copper oxychloride. Spraying of Axostrobin and tebuconazole @ 1.5-2 
g/L of water can be done. Varieties like Himgiri, Rio De Janeiro can be sown during 
June month.
