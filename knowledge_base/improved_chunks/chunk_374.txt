Castor
For optimal growth, sow the latest improved hybrid seed with a seed treatment of 
Azotobacter (5 ml per kg of seed). The recommended seed rate is 4 kg/ha, with a 
spacing of 45 cm × 10 cm. Apply a fertilizer dose of 80:40:0 NPK (kg/ha) along with 
10 tons of FYM per hectare to ensure healthy plant development.
