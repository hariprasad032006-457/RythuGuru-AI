Recommended variety is MR-6, with a seed rate of 12.5 kg/ha, sown during June-July. 
Fertilizer application is 100:50:50 kg NPK/ha.  Azospirillum biofertilizer is applied. 
Line sowing is done at a spacing of 30 × 10 cm. The crop is grown under rainfed 
conditions with no intercrop. Hand weeding is the primary weed control method. 
Blast disease is managed by spraying Saaf at 2 g per litre of water. Stem borer is 
controlled using Coragen at 0.5 g per litre of water.
Maize

such as CP-818, Arjun, Dharma (G.H. 150125), Shrusti (G.H. 0727), and private 
hybrids. The seed rate varies between 15 to 25 kg per hectare depending on the 
hybrid and season. Sowing is done from May to July for Kharif and from September to 
Pest management involves several strategies. For stem borer control, clipping the 
terminal leaf during transplanting is recommended, along with spraying
