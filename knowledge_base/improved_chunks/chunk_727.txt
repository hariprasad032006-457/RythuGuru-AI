Banana
palm includes 500 g Nitrogen, 320 g Phosphorus, and 1200 g Potassium. Cocoa is a 
common intercrop. Micronutrients like boron and zinc are applied. Drip irrigation is 
used. Weed management is done by hand weeding. Stem bleeding is managed by root 
feeding with Hexaconazole. Black-headed caterpillar is managed through the release of 
Goniozus nephantidis, a biological control agent.
Cashew

planting time for Vengurla types is between June and October, while Ullal and Chintamani
