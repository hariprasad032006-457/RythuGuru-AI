Field	sanitization:	Phyto-sanitary measures to be done which includes the removal 
and destruction of dried and diseased vines, fallen leaves, and any plant debris that 
may harbour pathogens. Thoroughly removing the litter from the base of pepper 
before the rains will prevent the diseases such as foot rot.
