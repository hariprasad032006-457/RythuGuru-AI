LIVESTOCK	MANAGEMENT
In Livestock shed use of 0.01 % Deltamethrin spraying for control of ecto and endo 
parasites 15 days intervals. Provide ample amount of clean and cold water to 
livestock to avoid Colitis disease. Use of white paint lime to drinking water tank. 
Vaccination of livestock should be done twice in a year against FMD. Livestock 
deworming should be done once in a six month. In case of buﬀalo wallowing should 
be done in summer season for enhancing the milk yield and to maintain the body 
temperature. Grooming of milch animal to avoiding the falling of loose hairs in the 
milk and to get the clean milk production and enhancing the blood circulation.
