Turmeric
Harvesting, boiling, drying, polishing operations are being carried out in turmeric crop. 
To save the crop from unseasonal rains or hailstorm, crop should not be stored in open 
place. The harvested turmeric crop should be covered by plastic paper so that it should 
not be damaged by rains.
Animal	Protection
To avoid leaf curl infestation in chilli transplanting of seedlings should be done 
before 30th June. Pest resistant or tolerant varieties like like Pusa Jwala or Pusa Jyoti 
should be used for planting.
