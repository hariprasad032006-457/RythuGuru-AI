Goat
Animals should be vaccinated before monsoon season for Enterotoxaemia and Peste 
des petes (PPR) ruminants' diseases to avoid great losses. Improved breeds like 
Osmanabadi should be used for higher income. Regular deworming should be done 
after every month to goat kids for six months for good health and weight gain.
In the summer the poultry shed should be kept cool. Plants more trees around the 
shed. The roof of the shed should be covered with grass, so that the temperature 
remains under control. Exhaust fan should be kept in the shed, so that the hot air 
inside shed is thrown out. Use foggers, so the shade will cool down. Improved 
backyard poultry breeds like Grampriya, Shrinidhi, Kaveri, Giriraja and Vanaraja 
should be used to boost up the income of small and marginal farmers.
